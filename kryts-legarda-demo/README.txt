1. Unzip this folder

This is a design prototype for your feedback - it is not a working website.
The shop, cart, checkout, search and contact form all show how they will
behave, but nothing is saved and no order is ever placed.

IMPORTANT: all products, prices and stock numbers are SAMPLE DATA that we
wrote ourselves to fill the layout. They are NOT a price list and they are
NOT a quote. Send us your real items and prices and we will put them in.

The yellow lines in the footer and contact page are blanks waiting for your
real phone number, exact address and shop hours.

Tell us what to change - colours, wording, product order, anything - and we
will update this before development starts.
