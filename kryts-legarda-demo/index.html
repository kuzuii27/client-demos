    2

    Mga madalas itanong

    Magkano ang shipping?

    This prototype shows a flat sample rate. On the live site we will plug in the real J&T and LBC rates per province, or Lalamove for same-day Metro Manila.

    Paano ko malalaman kung kasya ang bracket sa motor ko?

    Every bracket listing names the models it fits. If your model is not listed, message us — we fabricate custom brackets sa shop.

    May warranty ba ang helmet at top box?

    Replacement for manufacturing defects within 7 days, unused and in the original box. Final terms to be confirmed by the shop owner.

    Pwede ba akong mag-pick up sa Legarda?

    Yes. Choose “Pick up sa shop” at checkout and we will set your items aside.

    Accepting pa rin ba kayo ng Home Credit?

    In-store, yes. Whether Home Credit appears as an online payment option is one of the decisions for the next round.

    About Kryts Legarda

    Kryts Legarda has been serving riders along Legarda Street in Sampaloc, Manila as an automotive parts store built on one promise: the lowest price on top boxes, brackets and helmets.

    With more than 290,000 followers and a 96% recommendation rating from our customers, most of our orders still start as a comment or a PM. This website exists so you can order any time — prices in plain sight, stock on the page, and delivery nationwide.

    We are an authorized dealer of SEC Motosupply and we carry AccentWire and BRISK parts. Bracket fitting is free when you buy a top box and have it installed sa shop.
