01

General Contracting

Full-scope execution from site mobilisation to turnover, with our own formwork, rebar and finishing crews.

02

Project Management

Owner-side programme, cost and quality control when you are running multiple contractors.

Capabilities

Services

We take on work where engineering judgement matters more than the lowest bid. If a project is purely price-driven, we will tell you early.

01

General Contracting

Lump-sum or cost-plus execution of architectural and structural works. We self-perform concrete, rebar, formwork, masonry and finishing — which is why our programmes hold.

02

Design–Build

Our in-house structural team works with your architect from concept, so constructability problems get solved on paper instead of on site. One contract, one programme.

03

Structural Retrofit

Assessment, modelling and strengthening of existing buildings: column and beam jacketing, new shear walls, CFRP wrapping, foundation underpinning. Most of our retrofit work is done in occupied buildings.

04

Project & Construction Management

Owner-side representation: programme control, cost monitoring, variation assessment and quality inspection across multiple contract packages.

05

Site Development

Earthworks, slope protection, drainage, pavement and utilities for subdivision and industrial estates.

06

Fit-out & Renovation

Office, clinic and retail fit-out delivered on compressed night and weekend schedules in occupied buildings.
